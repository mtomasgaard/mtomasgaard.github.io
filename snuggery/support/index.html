Snuggery for iOS

Snuggery is a private, offline home for the mini-apps and files you make with AI. Everything stays on your device — there is no account to lose access to and no server to be down.

Common questions

A mini-app I imported shows a blank screen

It is almost always trying to load something from the internet — a font, a chart library, an image on a server. Mini-apps in Snuggery run fully offline and cannot make network requests, by design. Ask your AI to rebuild it with everything included in the ZIP and no external links.

My ZIP was refused

Snuggery inspects an archive before extracting anything, and refuses structures that are unsafe or unreasonably large. The message tells you which check failed. Most often the fix is to re-export it as a plain ZIP with the app's files at the top level, rather than a folder inside a folder.

Can I edit the data my apps use?

Yes — that is the point. Open a mini-app, tap ⋯, then App Files. Edit a .csv or .json file there and the app shows your change the next time it reads it. Try it with Run Tracker's runs.csv.

How do I get my work out?

Any item can be exported as a ZIP from its ⋯ menu, at any time, whether or not you have bought Snuggery. Nothing you make is ever locked inside the app.

I got a new phone

Purchases restore through Settings → Restore Purchases. Your library travels with an encrypted device backup or a direct device-to-device transfer.

Do I need a subscription?

No. Snuggery is one payment, once. You get the complete app free for seven days first, and when the trial ends, everything already in your library stays openable and exportable.

Does Snuggery build the apps for me?

No. Snuggery writes a prompt you paste into your own AI — Claude, ChatGPT, or any other — and imports and runs what comes back. The building happens in the AI you already use.

Privacy

Snuggery collects nothing: no accounts, no analytics, no servers. Full policy: Privacy Policy.
