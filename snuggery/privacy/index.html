Privacy Policy

Snuggery for iOS

Last updated: 29 August 2026

Snuggery does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data.

Everything you import into Snuggery — your files, your folders, and the mini-apps you run — stays on your device. Snuggery has no servers, no account system, and no analytics. Nothing you put in the app is sent anywhere.

Mini-apps

Mini-apps run inside Snuggery are blocked from reaching the internet. They can read and write only their own files, inside their own sandbox, and cannot see each other's data or the rest of your library.

Purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. Snuggery receives only whether a purchase was made; it never sees your payment details, and it has no way to identify you.

Network

Snuggery works fully offline and makes no network requests of its own.

Children

Snuggery is rated 4+ and is safe for any age. Because it collects no data at all, it collects no data from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. Snuggery collecting nothing is a design decision rather than a current setting, so a change here would mean a change to the product, announced in its release notes.
