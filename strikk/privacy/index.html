Privacy Policy

Strikk for iOS

Last updated: 8 September 2026

Strikk does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data.

Everything you put into Strikk — your projects, photos, yarn stash, and patterns, whether typed by hand or digitised from a photo or PDF — stays on your device. Strikk has no servers, no account system, and no analytics of any kind. Nothing you enter is sent anywhere.

Pattern digitisation and AI features

Turning a pattern photo or PDF into a structured, readable pattern, and resizing a pattern to your own gauge, run entirely on your iPhone using Apple's on-device Intelligence. Your pattern text and photos are never uploaded, and no part of this process involves a server of ours — because we do not have one.

What you choose to share

Two features can send information off your device, and only when you deliberately choose to use them, only to a destination you already use, and only after showing you exactly what would leave first:

  • Finding patterns online opens your own web browser with a search you can review before you go — Strikk builds the search, your browser runs it under your own identity.
  • Asking an AI chat to help read a pattern shows you the exact text first, then copies it to your clipboard and opens your own AI chat app — whichever one you already use — for you to paste into. Strikk never talks to any AI service directly, and never sees what comes back until you bring it back yourself.

Outside of those two features, which you have to start on purpose, Strikk makes no network connection of its own.

Camera and photos

Strikk uses the camera to photograph yarn labels and printed pattern pages. Photos are read on your device and never uploaded. Choosing a photo from your library works the same way — Strikk only ever sees the one photo you pick.

Purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. Strikk receives only whether Strikk Pro was purchased; it never sees your payment details, and it has no way to identify you.

Network

Strikk works fully offline. Beyond the two features described above, which you start yourself, it makes no network requests of its own.

Children

Strikk contains no objectionable content, and because it collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. Strikk collecting nothing is a design decision rather than a current setting, so a change here would mean a change to the product, described honestly rather than folded quietly into an update.
