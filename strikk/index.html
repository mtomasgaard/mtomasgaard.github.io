Strikk

Your knitting workspace.

Strikk is a calm, private workspace for the knitting you actually do. Count your rows. Keep your projects, with photos and the notes you scribble mid-row. Know what is in your yarn stash without digging through the cupboard. Read your patterns in a form built for knitting from, not for printing.

Everything works offline, because that is where knitting happens — on the sofa, on the train, in a cabin with no signal.

Free, with no catch

  • Unlimited row, repeat and stitch counters
  • Unlimited yarn and needle stash
  • Write and read your own patterns
  • Up to three projects
  • Import and keep your pattern PDFs and photos

Strikk Pro — one payment, yours forever

  • Turn a pattern PDF or photo into a structured, readable pattern
  • Resize a pattern to your own gauge, with every changed number explained
  • "What can I make?" — scores your patterns against the yarn you own
  • Unlimited projects

No subscription. No adverts. No account. No tracking. Strikk never connects to the internet on its own — pattern digitisation and resizing run entirely on your iPhone using Apple Intelligence. Nothing is uploaded, and nothing is read by anyone but you.
