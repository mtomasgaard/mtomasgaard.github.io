Common questions

Why didn't my pattern's chart come through?

Strikk does not read charts (colourwork or cable grids) into structured data yet — it recognises where a chart is referenced and tells you so rather than guessing at what it shows. Work from the original pattern for the charted sections; everything else in the pattern digitises normally.

A row in my digitised pattern looks cut off

Strikk is conservative on purpose: if it can't read a row with confidence, it flags it for review rather than guessing at a stitch count. The pattern-review screen is the same screen as the manual editor, so you can fix any row by hand — check the original page for that row's flagged content.

Do I need a subscription?

No. Strikk Pro is one payment, once. Unlimited counters, unlimited yarn stash, manual recipes, and up to three projects are free forever, with no catch.

I bought Pro on another device — how do I get it back?

Settings → Restore Purchases. Strikk doesn't keep its own record of whether you've bought Pro; it asks Apple every time, so restoring always reflects your real purchase history.

Does digitising a pattern or resizing it need the internet?

No. Both run entirely on your iPhone using Apple's on-device Intelligence. Nothing is uploaded. The only two things in Strikk that can involve leaving the app at all — searching for patterns online, and asking an AI chat to help read a pattern — open your own browser or your own chat app, and only when you choose to use them. Can I use Strikk without buying Pro?

Yes — the free tier is a complete, usable app: unlimited counters, unlimited yarn and needle stash, your own hand-written or manually entered patterns, and up to three projects. Pro adds automatic pattern digitisation, gauge-based resizing, the stash matcher, and unlimited projects.
